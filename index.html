Tuned per WCMesh whitepapers to reduce packet collisions; reviewed weekly.

The ranking strip uses CoreScope’s own usefulness grade and score, and rank among CoreScope repeaters by usefulness and by relays in the last 24 hours. Neighbour rank (all-time) is CoreScope’s rank by neighbour count among all nodes with edges. Heard and two-way counts use CoreScope’s last 7 days (two-way = bidirectional links with at least 20 packets). Farthest link is shown only when CoreScope has a distance. Uptime 7d is CoreScope availability. Weekly ▲▼ is the change in relays/24h from the Monday 8:05 AM PT baseline. Radio telemetry comes from the hourly repeater login when it is available. Every numeric metric the repeaters report is charted below; packet counters are cumulative since boot, so they are charted as per-hour rates between polls, and an interval is skipped when a repeater rebooted (uptime or a counter went down). Collision ratios use the same intervals: receive error ratio = errors ÷ (received + errors), flood duplicate ratio = flood dups ÷ flood received.
